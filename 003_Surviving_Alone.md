# Equipment
## Durability
Most equipment will break upon reaching 0 durability
- Most pieces of hand items have a per-use durability
- Clothing has a equipped-duration based durability
  - Clothing items and a select few other items can also be repaired via a `Sewing Kit`. Sewing kits usually repair a fixed number/duration of use. They also work on thermal stones.

## Armor
- Armor has a health value that they'll protect you from along with a % damage reduction
  - these will also break upon hitting 0%
Common options:
- Log suits and football helmets have the same amount of health and both provide 80% dr
  - the eye mask functions similarly but can be fed to restore durability
- Marble suits provide 90% dr but has substantially less health 
- If you wear multiple pieces of armor, you'll receive the highest level of dr, with the damage split across the armor in proportion to their dr

# Combat
## Spider hunting
- in the daytime you can bait them out from their nest, they are stunlockable
- if you attack them while on the webs, everyone will come out
- if you attack a spider and there are other ones nearby, they will also aggro
- only destroy nests when they are tier 3
- Tier 2 and Tier 3 nests
  - These nests will contain warrior spiders. They will come out if you hit a spider while on the webs or when you attack the nest. In general, we don't suggest fighting them head on.
  - spiders can be caught in traps, use traps to deal warrior spiders
  - Tier 3 nests can spawn spider queens, just leave them alone and they will eventually replant themselves.

## Hound kiting
- bait out an attack, hit 2x, dodge, hit twice dodge
- if you have a spear, hit 2x, dodge, hit 3x
- with more than one, try to run in a medium/large circle; they'll likely group up and you can fight them together/kite them as one hound
  - if they don't group up, their extra barking and getting distracted might also separate them to your advantage
    - if you want to take advantage of this, running straight is probably the better move
    - do be careful about them destroying/killing difficult-to-get/renew entities

## Pigs
- Feeding them meat makes them a follower for a bit
  - they will fight with/for you as well as chop trees, they are not great fighters
- Feeding them 4 monster meat converts them into a werepig
  - werepigs have a guaranteed drop of one pig butt and two large meat
  - you can keep attacking them for free for a bit when they're howling

## Nightmare Creatures
Crawling horrors and Terrorbeaks are not that different, just that one is faster.  
With low lag:
- hold attack so that you walk towards and swing
- as soon as the swing is over, dodge
- hit a second time and hold hit in case they spawn right on top of you
- repeat

With some lag:
- just dodge the first attack and then hold attack to hit
- if they spawn onto you, keep attacking
- if far away, reset and repeat

## Tree Guards
- they're pretty easy to kite and fight
- drops living logs and monster meat
- Poison birchnut trees and birchnutters are a bit more of a hassle, chop them down if you want to take them down; otherwise, they despawn when unloaded eventually
  - I usually just avoid chopping birch trees unless i'm really looking for birchnuts

# Food

## Crock Pot
- Filler: generally things that are either already in the recipe or berries or a veggie
  - ice is also often a filler; wait for mini-glaciers to be large before mining for maximum ice
- Common recipes:
  - Hunger:
    - meatballs: meat + 3 filler
    - omelette: meat + 3 egg; not as good as meatballs
  - Health:
    - butter muffin (20 health): butterfly wing + veggie + stick + stick (or more wings/veggies instead of stick)
    - pierogies (40 health): meat + egg + veggie + filler 
  - Sanity:
    - Sanity recipes tend to be sweets which can be harder to come by
    - alternatively, you can: 
      - sleep in a **tent** (which both heals and increases sanity at the cost of hunger)
      - eat cooked green mushrooms, cooked blue mushrooms, cooked cactus

## Mushrooms
Mushrooms count as veggie in the crock pot. All raw caps give 12.5 hunger. All cooked caps give 0 hunger.
