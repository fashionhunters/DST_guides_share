# Gameplay

## 1. Spawn
- This is the starting biome. It usually has some flowers, some grass, some twigs
- We come out of the <u>Florid Postern</u>. Try not to turn the map so that you know where "north" is
- In general, try not to pick flowers
  - it is not very sustainable or valuable
- Listen to character quotes except for hunger warnings
  - If you're not sure what is meant by a voice line, ask
- **If you don't know something, really don't be shy about asking, we wouldn't necessarily expect you to just figure it out, we only know it from playing so long**

## 2. Pre-Exploration
- Pick some grass, twigs and flint
- Take a look at the crafting tab
  - most importantly, locate "torch", "axe", "pickaxe"
  - additionally "campfire", "fire pit"
  - if you are in complete darkness (such as at night without any light, you *will* take damage)
- Crafting
  - Depending on the amount of flint you have, you might want to craft a pickaxe before an axe so you can get more flint
  - note that it is more important to have resources for a torch
- Learn how to punch a butterfly
  - force attack near one; they do somewhat fly away; feel free to wait for them to do a little hover/flutter-around
  - they are a good convenient source of food and healing
- The map is generally shaped like one big connected landmass, so we usually pick a coast and go in opposite direction to each take half and meet up to have a sense of where resources are
  - in a medium sized world, we'll probably meet up around day 7
- If something seems dangerous or starts attacking, *run*. Running away is almost always an option

## 3. Exploration
- Pick some carrots and berries as you go around for food
  - Carrots spoil more slowly than berries
  - Spoiled food give less stats, orange is usually still edible, red usually isn't worth
  - there isn't really a reason to pick too much as you'll have too much more than you can eat in a timely manner
- Precrafting
  - Instead of placing structures that you're about to craft, right click and it will still consume the resources and can be placed later
- learn about biomes as you go around (reference guide will be in 003_Surviving_Alone)

## 4. Night 1
- If there are lone trees near things to do, you can set a tree on fire for light while you work
- otherwise, keep exploring

## 5. More Exploration
- Good general rule of thumb: always have food, light, armor, and a weapon with you

- Science: prototyping (crafting) new recipes you didn't have access to before gives you some sanity, but is not a sustainable way to gain sanity (ie only useful as an incidental)
  - log suit
  - spear
  - alchemy engine (only one needed for base)
- Spiders
  - in the daytime you can bait them out from their nest, they are stunlockable
  - if you attack them while on the webs, everyone will come out


## 6. Meet up - First Base
General base items
- Alchemy Engine: more science
- Ice Box: slow spoilage (harder to get but you can also luck across some of the harder to get ingredients)
- Fire Pit
- light (and heat)
- Bird cage: feed meat to bird to get eggs
- Farm: fun veggies, mostly cook over a fire to eat (eat corn raw), cooked potato and cooked toma root heals
- Some crock pots

Cooking  
- Cooking food in a crock pot often gives food that yields more/better stats than the ingredients alone, sometimes even yielding special effects
- it also somewhat refreshes the spoilage of the food
- Definition: Filler: these are generally things that are either already in the recipe or berries or a veggie
- Common recipes:
  - Hunger:
    - meatballs: meat + 3 filler
    - omelette: meat + 3 egg; not as good as meatballs
  - Health:
    - butter muffin (20 health): butterfly wing + veggie + stick + stick (or more wings/veggies instead of stick)
    - pierogies (40 health): meat + egg + veggie + filler 
  - Sanity:
    - Sanity recipes tend to be sweets which can be harder to come by
    - alternatively, you can: 
      - sleep in a **tent** (which both heals and increases sanity at the cost of hunger)
      - eat cooked green mushrooms, cooked blue mushrooms, cooked cactus (you lose a small bit of health with the mushrooms)

The caves and lightbulbs
- lightbulbs can be used to make lanterns, which is a very nice source of lighting, however, the caves have its own host of challenges
- most caves landings have some lightbulbs, use those

## 7. Woof
Hounds will come periodically on the overworld to attack you. Another reason to always have armor and weapons on you. Once you can handle them, it is free food delivery.

Kiting pattern:
- bait out an attack, hit 2x, dodge, hit twice dodge
- if you have a spear, hit 2x, dodge, hit 3x


## 8. Prep for small boss
- Preparation
  - spear
  - log suits
  - healing
- Fight
  - dodge attacks
  - manage minions
- Reward: refillable log suit for your head as long as you don't let it break

## 9. Prep for big boss
- Preparation
  - hambat preferred
  - marble suit, log suit, football helmets(2)
  - clear out hound mounds
  - make a wall
- Fight
  - hold attack until she flies away to the pools (wearing marble suit + football helm)
  - run around the wall to the other side (switch to logsuit while doing this since marble suit has movement penalty)
  - wait for her to come back and keep attacking (switch back to marble)
  - when she gets mad, we will run away in a direction, pan flute, heal up, keep attacking
- Reward
  - scales
  - gems

## 10. Preparing for Winter
- in the winter it will be cold, you will want a thermal stone, some winter clothing (usually a winter hat, don't use rabbit earmuffs they suck)
- have a sustainable firewood farm as well as source of food, berries will stop growing (ice will appear, which is filler)

# Last Pointers
- Some of the larger encounters you'll very likely not be ready for, so just focus on surviving with the basic tools and amenities
- Remember, you can almost always run.
- A bite, a light, be prepared to fight (I think this is a Jazzy quote)
- try to carry a telltale heart with you to get revived and build up a supply of them
  - if you get revived via a telltale heart, your max hp will be reduced, make a booster shot to restore
- Getting comfortable farming spiders can take you far because they provide silk, food, and spider glands
