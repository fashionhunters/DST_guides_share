# Introduction

There is a lot of content in this game and it can be overwhelming. It is very likely that you will die a lot.

## Settings
- Lag compensation OFF
  - ON means the game tries to predict where you are, which means it can actually be wrong
  - OFF means you see what the game actually sees, including delayed inputs, but you'll know where you (and threats) actually are and can better attempt to dodge them
- Backpack INTEGRATED
  - determines whether your backpack slots are at the bottom of the screen with your inventory vs separate on the right side of the screen
  - mostly a matter of taste, but no harm in getting used to having everything in one place 

## Important Controls (default)
- Movement (WASD)
- Interact (space)
- Attack (F)
- Map (M)
- Mouse left click - usually for picking things up and moving around
- Mouse right click - usually for "use" or "consume"
- Modifiers (ctrl, alt, shift)
  - various things like taking half stacks, dropping on ground, moving between containers


## Stats, HUD, and Dying
Top right:
- Day Clock: shows time of day
  - yellow is day, orange is evening, blue is night
- Stats (you can hover over the respective symbols for numbers)
  - Hunger (Stomach)
    - Drains over time, when hunger is at 0, you will start draining health instead
  - Sanity (Brain) 
    - Drains in evening and night as well as with various actions and horrific things
    - When below 15%, nightmare creatures may start attacking you
  - **Health (Heart)**
    - Reach 0 and you <u>**die**</u>

Left: Crafting menu  
This is divided into multiple tabs, has favorites, you can set shorcuts, and is searchable

Bottom: Inventory  
Your inventory is along the bottom along with your three equip slots (hand, head, body)

## Character Selection
We think that it is important that you learn how to deal with the challenges in the game with default tools to build a solid foundation. We strongly recommend you play Wilson until you can survive the second autumn comfortably.

Other characters that don't stray overly far from the "default" experience (but still not super recommended until later) are:

- Willow
  - has a lighter to help with early darkness and cooking food over a fire
  - has Bernie the teddy bear that can help you with sanity challenges (but you should learn how to deal with it yourself)
- Winona
  - has a number of machines that you shouldn't deal with yet
  - when >50 hunger, drain hunger to craft faster
  - when <=50 hunger, crafts slower
